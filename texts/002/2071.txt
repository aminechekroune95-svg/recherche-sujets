2. Expression écrite (10 points)

Sujet A

media post in which you explain what costume you will wear and how it connects with
the cause you want to fight for.

Sujet B
How far does a work of fiction (novels, films, TV series, etc.) reflect reality or the
evolution of society? Discuss, using examples drawn from the English-speaking world.
